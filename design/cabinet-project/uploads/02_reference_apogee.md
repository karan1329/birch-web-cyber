# Reference 02 · "Apogee" AI predictive hero image

**Source:** Dribbble shot 27295783, *AI Predictive SaaS Landing Page*, by Kris Anfalova. Observed directly, captured at intervals across the animation cycle. Teardown for study, not a template to copy.

The designer's own note on this one is unusually explicit and worth holding onto: she "stepped away from the standard tech-blue colour palettes" and went for "a premium dark mode aesthetic with cinematic, deep-red celestial and rocket-launch imagery," enhanced by "subtle grain textures and an elegant serif typography system," in order to ground the product "in a sense of awe, ambition, and stability."

That sentence is the whole method. The image is doing emotional work, and the dashboard is doing evidential work, and they are kept in separate parts of the composition.

---

## 1. Structure

Unlike Axiom, this hero is **not split**. It is a single full-bleed image field with the type centred on top of it and the product UI floating in the lower half. The nav sits over the image, transparent.

The hero **cycles between two distinct image states**. Captured seconds apart, they are different compositions, not a crossfade of the same one. Treat them as two separate frames of one art direction.

---

## 2. State A, the celestial arc

**Ground:** near-black, warm, roughly `#0D0806` to `#160C09`. Not a neutral black. There is red in the shadow.

**Subject:** an enormous circle rising from below the frame like a sun at the horizon, cropped by the bottom edge, occupying roughly the middle 60 percent of the width. It is a soft-edged coral to red field, brightest at the lower centre, falling off to nothing before it reaches the top of the frame.

Approximate values:

| role | approx |
|---|---|
| ground | `#0D0806` |
| arc outer falloff | `#3A1410` |
| arc body | `#B8402F` |
| arc core | `#E2604A` to `#F07A5E` |
| type and UI text | `#F5EFEA` |

**Texture:** heavy, visible film grain across the entire frame, coarser in the shadows. This is doing enormous work. It unifies the flat vector arc, the photographic-feeling ground, and the crisp UI mockup into one surface. Without grain, a soft red circle on black looks like a CSS radial gradient. With grain, it looks photographed.

**Vignette:** the corners fall to near-pure black. The frame is darkest at the four corners and brightest at the lower centre.

**Type:** three lines of high-contrast serif, centred, white, sitting in the upper-middle where the arc is dimmest. Above it a small translucent pill with a tiny dot and mono-ish label reading `Live Data Stream`.

**Product:** below the type, a **browser-chrome mockup** floats: traffic-light dots, an address bar, a tabbed app inside. It is tinted into the coral, its own contrast reduced so it belongs to the image rather than sitting on top of it. Inside: a tabbed header reading `Global · Cluster · Insights`, then a row of KPI cards showing a large money figure with a percentage delta, a dot-matrix chart, a small bar chart, then a second row with a log panel and a trajectory panel with a percentage.

Note the same trick as Axiom: **the UI is desaturated and tinted into the image's hue** so it reads as part of the scene.

---

## 3. State B, the launch

**Subject:** a photographic landscape at dusk. Layered mountain ridges in near-black silhouette across the lower third, a heavy cloud bank filling the middle, warm grey to dun in colour, and a **single vertical beam of coral light rising from the horizon at dead centre**, thin, with a small bright point at its top.

The beam is the only saturated element in an otherwise desaturated frame. Everything else is warm grey, olive-black, dun.

**Composition:** rigidly symmetric. The beam is on the vertical centreline. The horizon is low, roughly the lower third. The eye goes up the beam and stops at the point.

**Type:** three lines of the same serif, centred, white, sitting over the mountains in the lower half where the image is darkest and least detailed. Above the type, a small translucent pill reading `Backed by:` and a mark.

**Grain and vignette:** same treatment as State A, slightly heavier.

---

## 4. What makes it work

**One accent against a desaturated field.** Coral is the only chroma in either frame. Everything else is black, warm grey, or white. This is the same discipline as Axiom's single blue, executed on a dark ground.

**Grain as the unifier.** Three different kinds of element (vector arc, photograph, crisp UI) end up on one surface because a single grain layer sits over all of them. This is the cheapest and most effective move in the entire reference set.

**The type sits in the quiet.** In both states the headline is placed exactly where the image has the least detail and the lowest contrast. Nothing is scrimmed, nothing has a text shadow, nothing needs a dark overlay box. The image was composed to leave a hole for the words.

**Monumentality by symmetry.** Dead-centre subject, low horizon, cropped-by-frame scale. The subject is implied to be much larger than the frame. That is what produces "awe" rather than "nice picture."

**Emotional frame, evidential product.** The image carries feeling. The dashboard carries proof. They never fight because they occupy different halves of the composition and the dashboard has been tinted down into the image.

---

## 5. What to leave

- The coral and red palette. It is hers, it is loud, and it reads as consumer AI.
- Rocket and celestial imagery. Aspiration is the wrong register for a regulated-finance security buyer, who is not buying ambition, they are buying the absence of a bad surprise.
- Fabricated KPI dashboards with invented revenue figures. On a page selling security assessment to a bank, an invented number is a credibility liability, not an asset.
- Full-bleed type over photography as the primary hero. It works for a product with a large brand budget and no compliance reader.

---

## 6. The two shots compared

| | Axiom | Apogee |
|---|---|---|
| ground | white page, image in a panel | dark, image is the page |
| structure | asymmetric split | centred, symmetric |
| palette | one blue | one coral on near-black |
| type | uppercase sans with a serif italic line set between two sans lines | high-contrast serif, all lines |
| subject | object embedded in atmosphere | monumental form, low horizon |
| instrument device | dot-matrix contour overlay | the product UI itself, tinted in |
| unifier | value compression | film grain |
| product shown | small translucent cards | full browser mockup |

Both are by the same designer. Both obey the same three rules: **one hue, one unifying texture treatment, and negative space reserved for type before anything else is placed.**
